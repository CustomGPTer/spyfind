What SpyFind does not do

SpyFind cannot see a camera that has no wireless and is switched off, and on hotel or guest Wi-Fi that keeps devices apart the network scan sees less. It does not remove spyware for you, on purpose. Only AirTags, Find My items and Find Hub tags can be made to ring. It is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself.
