Release notes

What changed in each version of SpyFind. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.0 23 November 2026Latest

  • First release. Find hidden cameras, trackers and spyware, all on your phone.
  • Full Sweep: Bluetooth and Wi-Fi together, with the reason each device was flagged.
  • Bluetooth scan: cameras, earbuds, watches and trackers named, AirTags, SmartTags, Tile, Chipolo and Find Hub tags among them. A hot and cold finder with Geiger clicks, and play sound for AirTags and Find Hub tags.
  • Tracker Watch: off until switched on. Warns you if the same tracker is with you at several places. Locations are kept on the phone and deleted after 24 hours.
  • Wi-Fi camera scan: every device on the network, cameras marked, and nearby camera set-up hotspots.
  • Lens finder, infrared finder and magnetic scanner.
  • Spyware app scan against the Echap stalkerware list, with a risk score for every other app, and the list can be updated from Settings.
  • Camera and mic alert, off until switched on.
  • Room Sweep checklists, history, PDF reports, trusted devices, app lock, widget and Quick Settings tile, light and dark looks.
  • Guides on what to do if you find something, and free UK helplines.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.0 is called SpyFind.apk. It is 4.4 MB (4,359,257 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

b3a69167dfd3e992d999fa3a7eedda6435fc7aed97bc8582695cd215796f6427

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile SpyFind.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
